Quantum algebra, quantum topology and topological field theory

I am a doctoral researcher at the Institut de Mathématiques de Bourgogne in Dijon, working with Peter Schauenburg on orthogonality for group-theoretical fusion categories.

I am interested in the algebraic structures behind low-dimensional topology, and in how tensor categories produce topological field theories and invariants of manifolds. Before Dijon I studied physics at Zewail City and high energy physics at ICTP in Trieste, where I wrote a thesis on two-dimensional topological field theories.

Research interests

  • Fusion and modular tensor categories, in particular group-theoretical fusion categories
  • Topological quantum field theories and quantum invariants of 3-manifolds
  • Quantum groups and Hopf algebras
  • Factorization homology
