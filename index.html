Quantum algebra, quantum topology and topological field theory

I am a doctoral researcher at the Institut de Mathématiques de Bourgogne in Dijon. I am currently working on orthogonality for group-theoretical fusion categories.

Furthermore, I am interested in higher category theory and topological invariants, in particular how tensor categories and their higher analogues give rise to topological field theories. Much of my motivation comes from mathematical physics, where these structures appear as the symmetries, defects and observables of quantum field theories. I came to mathematics from physics, studying at Zewail City and then at ICTP in Trieste.

Research interests

  • Fusion and modular tensor categories
  • Topological quantum field theories
  • Quantum groups and Hopf algebras
  • Factorization homology
  • Higher category theory and topological invariants
  • Generalized global symmetries and defects in quantum field theory
