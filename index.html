About

I am a mathematician interested in the algebraic structures behind low-dimensional topology: fusion and modular tensor categories, topological quantum field theories and the quantum invariants of 3-manifolds, quantum groups, and factorization homology.

I am a doctoral researcher at the Institut de Mathématiques de Bourgogne in Dijon, working with Peter Schauenburg on orthogonality for group-theoretical fusion categories. Before that I completed the M2 in mathematical physics in Dijon, with a thesis on factorization homology supervised by Lukas Woike, and the postgraduate diploma in high energy physics at ICTP in Trieste.

Beyond my research I study BPS line defects and supersymmetric field theories, algebraic geometry, and homotopy theory. At IMB I organize the working group on stable homotopy theory and higher algebra.
